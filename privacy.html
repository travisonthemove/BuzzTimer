Privacy

Your buzz, your data.

BuzzTimer is designed to keep things simple and local:

  • No account is required.

    You don’t need a login to use BuzzTimer. It’s built as a single-page web app.

  • Browser storage and its limits.

    Settings, timer state, session details, and archived session history are kept in this browser’s local storage. There is no BuzzTimer server database or cloud backup. Current moments and ideas are kept in memory until the session is archived on Reset; reloading before that can lose those entries. Export important history before clearing browser data or changing devices.

    This storage is not encrypted by BuzzTimer. Someone with access to your browser profile, or other code on the same website origin, may be able to read it. Private browsing, storage restrictions, or browser cleanup can prevent saving or remove saved data.

  • Hosting and font requests.

    The app code does not send journal entries to an analytics service. GitHub Pages serves the site, and Google Fonts supplies the typeface; those services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Tooltip scripts are served with the app rather than loaded from a third-party script host. The app still works if font requests fail.

  • What you write is for you.

    Sharing, copying, screenshots, and exported files can disclose your entries. Review the sharing preview before sending it: Detailed Share can include the product name and logged moments. Choosing a social sharing button or an entertainment link opens another service with its own privacy practices.

BuzzTimer is not a secure medical record system and shouldn’t be used as one. Avoid entering sensitive personal details that you wouldn’t be comfortable having on your device.

If a future version adds features like cloud backup, accounts, or optional analytics, this Privacy section will be updated in clear language so you can make an informed choice.
